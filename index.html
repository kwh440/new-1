Welcome to travel Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka, officially the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka and formerly known as Ceylon, is a vibrant island nation in South Asia located in the Indian Ocean, just southwest of the Bay of Bengal and separated from India by the Palk Strait. Home to roughly 22 million people, the country features a diverse multi-ethnic and multi-religious society—primarily comprising Sinhalese, Sri Lankan Tamils, and Sri Lankan Moors—with Colombo serving as its commercial and executive capital and Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte as its legislative capital. Known as the "Pearl of the Indian Ocean," Sri Lanka boasts a rich history spanning over three thousand years, a tropical climate, lush central highlands, and a celebrated cultural heritage rooted in Theravada Buddhism and ancient hydraulic civilizations.
